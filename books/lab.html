HOW TO TRAIN

Rules, Bands & the 7 Drill Types

📖 4 MIN

Training beats re-reading. Each exercise is solvable from Book 1 alone — chapter named on every header — and each solution teaches the reasoning, not just the answer.

📏 The three rules
  • Attempt before hint. Write something — even a wrong diff teaches.
  • Hint before solution. One nudge is usually enough.
  • No console until predicted. Plan on paper, then run.
🎚️ The bands
  • 🟢 Warm-up — one fact, 2 minutes.
  • 🟡 Drill — two ideas combined, 5–8 minutes.
  • 🔴 Boss — incident or refactor, 10–15 minutes.
